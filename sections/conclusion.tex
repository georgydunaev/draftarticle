\section{Conclusion and Future Directions}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We have presented a modular formalization of propositional and predicate logic in the Coq proof assistant. By structuring the formalization into distinct modules for syntax, Boolean reflection, axiomatic deduction, and verification tests, the development remains extensible and maintainable.

Future work includes extending the deductive system to full Morse-Kelley and Zermelo-Fraenkel set theories \cite{jech1978settheory, werner1997zfc}, incorporating automated cut-elimination procedures \cite{girard1972thesis}, and verifying completeness theorems within higher-order categorical models.
