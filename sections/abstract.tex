\begin{abstract}
This research paper presents the formal implementation and verification of mathematical foundations within the Coq proof assistant. We formalize propositional logic and first-order predicate logic, encompassing Boolean reflection, double-negation semantics, classical models, and Kripke semantics. Building on established formulations of constructive and axiomatic logic \cite{vereshchagin2002lectures, krupski2019coq}, our formalization addresses modular proof engineering, syntactic encodings, and verified metatheoretic properties. We show that foundational deductive systems can be embedded concisely while maintaining proof automation and mechanical checkability within modern interactive theorem proving workflows \cite{coq2024refman, werner1997zfc}.
\end{abstract}
